# Legal Boundary

## Non-negotiable Rules

1. Do not scrape or automate extraction from third-party websites without explicit written permission.
2. Do not copy proprietary texts, images, rankings, or editorial content.
3. Use original content or properly licensed partner content.
4. Keep source attribution records for all imported data.
5. Ensure user-uploaded photos pass moderation before public visibility.

## Data Policy for GastroVoyage MVP

- Build and maintain an internal dataset of dishes, restaurants, and dish-to-restaurant mapping for Baku.
- Write unique descriptions and metadata in-house.
- Store provenance fields (`source_type`, `source_note`, `verified_by`, `verified_at`) on curated records.

## Release Gate Checklist

- Legal review completed for all seed datasets.
- Copyright checks passed for all media.
- Terms of use and privacy policy published in app.
- Data deletion flow available for user-generated content.

## Sponsored Content Compliance

- Sponsored listings must be clearly marked as promoted.
- Editorial and sponsored ranking signals must remain distinguishable.
- User-generated media cannot be used in partner ads without consent.
